# 6-Week Interview Preparation Plan

This plan assumes a moderate pace for working professionals. Adjust according to available time and areas needing extra work.

## Week 1: Baseline & Fundamentals
- **Day 1:** Kickoff & Self-Assessment  
  - Take an initial mock interview (coding + behavioral) to identify strengths and weaknesses.  
  - Document your baseline scores in the mock interview scorecard template.
- **Day 2–3:** Data Structures Refresher  
  - Review arrays, linked lists, stacks, queues, hash maps, sets.  
  - Solve 3–4 problems per category using the coding framework.
- **Day 4:** Algorithm Basics  
  - Revisit sorting and searching algorithms (binary search, quicksort, mergesort).  
  - Analyze time and space complexities.
- **Day 5:** System Design Foundations  
  - Read system-design.md to understand the framework for problem breakdown.  
  - Sketch the architecture for a URL shortener or tiny cache service using the scratchpad.
- **Day 6–7:** Behavioral Stories Gathering  
  - Collect significant experiences from your past projects.  
  - Use the STAR template to draft at least two stories per theme: leadership, conflict resolution, innovation, and failure.  
  - Schedule a brief review with a peer to refine stories.

## Week 2: Core Problem Solving
- **Day 8–9:** Linked Lists & Trees  
  - Solve classic problems: reverse linked list, detect cycle, BST operations, traversals.  
  - Practice articulating approaches before coding.
- **Day 10:** Recursion & Backtracking  
  - Work through problems like combinations, permutations, and subset sums.  
  - Emphasize base cases, recursion trees, and pruning strategies.
- **Day 11–12:** System Design Primer  
  - Learn about scalability, consistency, availability, CAP theorem, caching.  
  - Read case studies of popular systems (e.g., Instagram feed).  
  - Document questions to ask in system design interviews.
- **Day 13:** Behavioral Deep Dive  
  - Refine STAR stories; add quantifiable outcomes.  
  - Practice concise 2–3 minute delivery for each story.
- **Day 14:** Review & Rest  
  - Summarize lessons from Week 2.  
  - Light exercise or hobby to recharge. Ensure enough sleep.

## Week 3: Intermediate Algorithms & System Design
- **Day 15–16:** Graphs & Trees  
  - Study BFS, DFS, Dijkstra’s, A*, topological sorting.  
  - Solve problems on shortest paths, connected components, and scheduling.
- **Day 17:** Dynamic Programming  
  - Review patterns (knapsack, longest common subsequence, matrix path).  
  - Solve at least two medium-level DP problems.
- **Day 18–19:** System Design: Data Storage & Databases  
  - Compare SQL vs NoSQL.  
  - Learn about partitioning, replication, sharding, indexing, and transactions.  
  - Design a simple key-value storage service.
- **Day 20:** Communication Practice  
  - Record yourself explaining algorithms and designs.  
  - Focus on clarity, pacing, and use of technical vocabulary.
- **Day 21:** Behavioral Simulations  
  - Pair up with a friend to simulate behavioral rounds.  
  - Obtain feedback on authenticity and structure.

## Week 4: Advanced Concepts & Mock Interviews
- **Day 22–23:** Concurrency & Multithreading  
  - Review concurrency primitives (locks, semaphores) and common pitfalls (deadlock, race conditions).  
  - Solve concurrency problems if relevant to your target roles.
- **Day 24–25:** Scaling & Distributed Systems  
  - Deep dive into load balancing, caching strategies, message queues, microservices vs monoliths.  
  - Design a scalable chat service or real-time analytics pipeline.
- **Day 26:** Full-Length Coding Mock Interview  
  - Conduct a timed coding interview with a peer or using an online platform.  
  - Use the scorecard to track performance and identify improvement areas.
- **Day 27:** Behavioral Story Expansion  
  - Create stories that demonstrate collaboration across teams, influencing without authority, and delivering under pressure.  
  - Practice tailoring stories to different company cultures.
- **Day 28:** Rest & Reflection  
  - Take a day off to avoid burnout.  
  - Reflect on progress and adjust the plan if necessary.

## Week 5: Holistic Preparation & Domain Knowledge
- **Day 29–30:** Domain-Specific Topics  
  - Depending on your industry, study relevant topics (e.g., machine learning basics for data roles, memory management for systems roles).  
  - Prepare at least one case study demonstrating domain expertise.
- **Day 31–32:** Systems at Scale  
  - Design complex systems such as a ride-sharing platform, a streaming video service, or an e-commerce checkout system.  
  - Focus on trade-offs among latency, throughput, and cost.
- **Day 33:** Behavioral & Cultural Fit  
  - Research target companies’ values.  
  - Map your experiences to those values and prepare closing questions to ask interviewers.
- **Day 34–35:** Multi-Round Mock Interviews  
  - Simulate a full-day interview loop with multiple rounds (coding, system design, behavioral).  
  - Take breaks between rounds to simulate real scheduling.  
  - Gather comprehensive feedback.

## Week 6: Final Polishing & Confidence Building
- **Day 36:** Weak Spot Remediation  
  - Review scorecards and feedback from previous weeks.  
  - Focus on 2–3 weak areas with targeted practice.
- **Day 37–38:** High-Pressure Mocks  
  - Schedule back-to-back interviews to build stamina.  
  - Practice stress management techniques (breathing, visualization).
- **Day 39:** Review All Notes & Templates  
  - Skim through coding frameworks, system design frameworks, and your behavioral stories.  
  - Consolidate cheat sheets for last-minute review.
- **Day 40:** Light Practice & Rest  
  - Tackle a couple of easy problems to stay warm.  
  - Ensure your devices, environment, and logistics are ready.
- **Day 41:** Final Mock & Mindset  
  - Perform one last comprehensive mock interview.  
  - Reflect on your journey; focus on confidence rather than cramming.
- **Day 42:** Relax & Prepare for Interview Day  
  - Engage in relaxing activities.  
  - Get plenty of sleep and maintain a positive mindset.

---

Remember that quality practice, reflection, and rest are more valuable than sheer volume. Customize this plan to fit your schedule and the specific requirements of the roles you are targeting.
