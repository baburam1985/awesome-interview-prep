# 8-Week Interview Preparation Plan

This extended plan is designed for candidates who want a thorough ramp-up over two months while balancing work and life commitments. Feel free to compress or expand parts based on your strengths and the roles you’re targeting.

## Week 1: Self-Assessment & Goal Setting
- **Day 1:** Baseline Mock Interviews  
  - Perform a full mock interview (coding + behavioral) to gauge initial readiness.  
  - Use the mock interview scorecard to record observations.  
  - Identify your top 3 focus areas (e.g., dynamic programming, system design, communication).
- **Day 2:** Goal Setting & Schedule Customization  
  - Define SMART goals for your interview journey (e.g., “solve 30 medium-level problems” or “master design of large-scale feed systems”).  
  - Adjust this 8-week plan’s pacing if you foresee time constraints.
- **Day 3–4:** Fundamentals Refresher  
  - Review data structure basics (arrays, strings, linked lists, hash maps, stacks, queues).  
  - Solve a few easy to medium problems to build momentum.
- **Day 5:** Study Patterns & Frameworks  
  - Read through coding.md and familiarize yourself with the problem-solving framework.  
  - Browse system-design.md and system-design-scratchpad.md to understand expectations.
- **Day 6–7:** Behavioral Story Inventory  
  - List out 8–10 significant career achievements and challenges.  
  - Categorize them by leadership, collaboration, problem solving, and failure.  
  - Draft outlines using the STAR method.

## Week 2: Data Structures Deep Dive
- **Day 8–9:** Arrays & Strings  
  - Tackle sliding window, two pointers, and string manipulation problems.  
  - Practice optimizing solutions from O(n²) to O(n) where possible.
- **Day 10–11:** Linked Lists & Stacks  
  - Solve problems involving reversal, detection of cycles, and implementing a stack/queue using a linked list.  
  - Review complexity trade-offs for each structure.
- **Day 12:** Queues & Priority Queues  
  - Study applications such as BFS and scheduling.  
  - Solve problems using heaps (e.g., top K elements).
- **Day 13–14:** Hash Maps & Sets  
  - Work through anagram grouping, two sum, and frequency counting problems.  
  - Discuss collision handling and resizing strategies.

## Week 3: Algorithms & Recursion
- **Day 15:** Recursion Fundamentals  
  - Review call stack mechanics, tail recursion, and identify when recursion is appropriate.  
  - Solve factorial, Fibonacci, and basic tree traversals.
- **Day 16–17:** Backtracking  
  - Practice N-Queens, Sudoku solver, and subset generation.  
  - Emphasize systematic exploration and pruning.
- **Day 18:** Divide & Conquer  
  - Work on merge sort, quick sort, and Karatsuba multiplication.  
  - Learn to identify subproblem structures.
- **Day 19–20:** Greedy Algorithms  
  - Study interval scheduling, Huffman coding, and coin change problems.  
  - Understand why greedy works (or doesn’t) through proof sketches.
- **Day 21:** Behavioral Refinement  
  - Develop your draft stories into polished narratives.  
  - Add metrics and impact statements.  
  - Practice delivering them succinctly.

## Week 4: Graphs & Dynamic Programming
- **Day 22–23:** Graph Traversal  
  - Implement BFS and DFS from scratch.  
  - Solve problems like number of islands, course schedule, and shortest path in unweighted graphs.
- **Day 24:** Weighted Graphs  
  - Learn Dijkstra’s and Bellman-Ford algorithms.  
  - Apply them to pathfinding problems.
- **Day 25–26:** Dynamic Programming Basics  
  - Tackle classic problems: Fibonacci memoization, climbing stairs, house robber.  
  - Focus on identifying overlapping subproblems and optimal substructure.
- **Day 27:** Advanced DP  
  - Solve longest increasing subsequence, edit distance, and coin change with unlimited coins.  
  - Try space optimization techniques.
- **Day 28:** Mock Coding Interview  
  - Conduct a timed medium-difficulty coding interview.  
  - Reflect on problem selection and time management.

## Week 5: System Design Fundamentals
- **Day 29:** Introduction to Distributed Systems  
  - Understand core concepts: scalability, consistency vs availability, CAP theorem.  
  - Study examples like distributed counters and leader election.
- **Day 30–31:** Data Storage & Persistence  
  - Compare relational and non-relational databases.  
  - Practice designing schema for a simple blogging platform.  
  - Discuss indexing, partitioning, and replication strategies.
- **Day 32:** Networking & Protocols  
  - Review HTTP, REST vs gRPC, WebSockets.  
  - Understand the importance of idempotency and statelessness.
- **Day 33–34:** Small System Design Exercises  
  - Design a URL shortener, rate limiter, or file storage service.  
  - Use the scratchpad template to document requirements, estimates, API, data model, and high-level design.
- **Day 35:** Behavioral Midpoint Check  
  - Revisit your behavioral stories; practice different permutations and follow-up questions.  
  - Seek feedback from someone who conducts interviews.

## Week 6: Scaling Up & Advanced Topics
- **Day 36–37:** High-Scale System Design  
  - Take on systems with heavy throughput (e.g., Twitter feed, ride-sharing matching service).  
  - Discuss sharding, caching, eventual consistency, and data pipelines.
- **Day 38:** Real-Time & Streaming Systems  
  - Learn about message queues (Kafka, RabbitMQ) and stream processors (Flink, Spark Streaming).  
  - Design a real-time analytics dashboard.
- **Day 39:** Security & Reliability  
  - Understand authentication, authorization, encryption, and rate limiting.  
  - Discuss disaster recovery, failover strategies, and graceful degradation.
- **Day 40:** Concurrency & Parallelism  
  - Study thread safety, mutexes, atomic operations.  
  - Solve producer-consumer or reader-writer problems.
- **Day 41:** Mock System Design Interview  
  - Simulate a 45–60 minute system design interview with a peer.  
  - Focus on requirement clarification, high-level design, component deep dive, and trade-off analysis.

## Week 7: Full-Length Practice & Domain Expertise
- **Day 42–43:** Full Mock Interview Days  
  - Conduct end-to-end interviews (coding + system design + behavioral) with peers or platforms.  
  - Rotate problem types and difficulty.  
  - Capture detailed feedback.
- **Day 44–45:** Domain-Specific Deep Dive  
  - Spend two days on domain knowledge relevant to your target roles (e.g., machine learning, operating systems, networking, or product design).  
  - Build a mini-project or case study to solidify understanding.
- **Day 46:** Leadership & Collaboration  
  - Prepare scenarios where you demonstrated ownership, collaboration, mentoring, or conflict resolution.  
  - Align these with the company values you’re interviewing for.
- **Day 47:** Communication Mastery  
  - Practice explaining complex topics to non-experts.  
  - Work on pacing, tone, and structure.
- **Day 48:** Reflection & Adjustment  
  - Review all feedback and identify persistent weak areas.  
  - Adjust final week focus accordingly.

## Week 8: Final Stretch & Confidence Building
- **Day 49–50:** Weakness Blitz  
  - Dedicate two days solely to your weakest topics (coding patterns, design nuances, or behavioral framing).  
  - Use targeted problem sets or design exercises.
- **Day 51–52:** High-Pressure Simulations  
  - Schedule back-to-back interviews to build stamina and time management.  
  - Practice relaxation techniques to manage stress.
- **Day 53:** Review & Consolidate  
  - Skim all your notes, templates, and frameworks.  
  - Prepare cheat sheets for last-minute reference.
- **Day 54:** Light Practice & Logistics  
  - Solve a couple of easy problems to stay sharp.  
  - Confirm your interview schedules, environment setup, and attire.
- **Day 55:** Final Mock Interview  
  - Conduct one last comprehensive mock interview with a trusted peer or mentor.  
  - Focus on confidence, clarity, and structured thinking.
- **Day 56:** Rest & Mental Preparation  
  - Engage in relaxing activities and avoid heavy study.  
  - Sleep well and maintain a positive mindset.

---

This 8-week plan is intentionally extensive, giving you time to internalize concepts, practice steadily, and iterate on feedback. Remember to balance discipline with rest, and adapt as needed to keep motivation high.
