# 2-Week Interview Prep Plan

This schedule is for candidates who have a short timeframe to prepare for interviews. It focuses on high-impact activities.

## Week 1

### Day 1-2: Assessment & Foundations
- Review the job description and understand the role requirements.
- Take a mock coding interview to assess your baseline.
- Identify top areas of improvement in algorithms, system design, and behavioral stories.

### Day 3-4: Coding Refresh
- Focus on solving 2–3 medium complexity problems per day on core topics (arrays, strings, hash maps, trees).
- Practice articulating your approach using the framework from `frameworks/coding.md`.
- Timebox each problem and conduct a brief retrospective.

### Day 5: System Design Primer
- Review system design basics: scalability, reliability, caching, database selection.
- Read through the `frameworks/system-design.md` and the `templates/system-design-scratchpad.md` to understand the structure.
- Design a simple system (e.g., URL shortener) and practice discussing your design.

### Day 6-7: Behavioral Stories
- Draft 3–4 STAR stories using the `templates/behavioral-stories.md`.
- Focus on different themes: leadership, conflict resolution, impact, learning from failure.
- Practice telling these stories out loud and get feedback from a peer.

## Week 2

### Day 8-9: Advanced Coding & Mock Interviews
- Tackle harder problems, focusing on dynamic programming, graphs, and trees.
- Schedule at least one mock coding interview with a peer or use an interview platform.
- Review mistakes and refine problem-solving approach.

### Day 10: Deep Dive System Design
- Select one complex system (e.g., messaging service, feed generation).
- Use the scratchpad to work through requirements, scale, API design, and component choices.
- Discuss trade-offs and non-functional requirements.

### Day 11: Behavioral Refinement
- Revisit your STAR stories and add quantifiable results.
- Prepare answers for common behavioral questions like “Tell me about yourself,” “Why this company?”, and “Describe a challenge you faced.”
- Record yourself to evaluate clarity and pacing.

### Day 12: End-to-End Mock Interview
- Conduct a full-length mock interview covering coding, system design, and behavioral questions.
- Ask for candid feedback and identify remaining gaps.

### Day 13: Review & Rest
- Summarize lessons learned from mock interviews.
- Light coding practice to reinforce patterns.
- Rest and ensure adequate sleep.

### Day 14: Final Prep & Mindset
- Skim notes, frameworks, and templates.
- Visualize success and maintain a calm mindset.
- Prepare logistics for interview day (technical setup, attire, space).
