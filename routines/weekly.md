# Weekly Interview Prep Plan  
  
This plan outlines a structured weekly routine to maintain momentum and balance across different interview preparation areas. Feel free to adjust days to suit your schedule.  
  
## Monday: Reflect & Review  
- Review notes from the previous week to identify strengths and gaps.  
- Revisit challenging algorithm solutions or system design concepts.  
- Update your study plan based on what you learned.  
  
## Tuesday: System Design Deep Dive  
- Choose one system design topic (e.g., caching strategies, message queues, database partitioning).  
- Sketch out architectures using the system‑design scratchpad template.  
- Research real-world implementations and trade-offs.  
- Write down questions you'd ask in an interview when exploring this topic.  
  
## Wednesday: Coding Practice  
- Solve 3‑4 medium to hard algorithm problems using your coding interview template.  
- Focus on clarity, efficiency, and testing your code.  
- After solving, revisit solutions and note any patterns or strategies.  
  
## Thursday: Behavioral Stories  
- Practice one STAR story from your experience.  
- Write it out using the behavioral stories template, highlighting the Situation, Task, Action, and Result.  
- Rehearse out loud to improve delivery and confidence.  
  
## Friday: Mock Interview / Peer Review  
- Conduct a mock interview with a peer or use an online platform.  
- Use the mock interview scorecard to evaluate performance across coding, system design, and behavioral questions.  
- Ask for constructive feedback and identify areas to improve.  
  
## Saturday: Light Learning & Relaxation  
- Spend 1‑2 hours reading technical blogs or watching system design videos.  
- Reflect on new insights without stressing about solving problems.  
- Ensure you rest and recharge to avoid burnout.  
  
## Sunday: Weekly Recap & Planning  
- Summarize what you accomplished during the week.  
- Update your templates and notes with new insights.  
- Plan next week's focus topics and schedule.  
- Celebrate progress and set goals for the coming week.  
  
---  
Adhering to a weekly cadence helps maintain a balanced approach to interview prep, ensuring consistent improvement across coding, system design, and behavioral skills.
