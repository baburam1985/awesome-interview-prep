# Daily Interview Prep Routine

A concise daily routine to sharpen your interview skills.

## Morning (30 min)  
- Quick coding warm-up: solve one easy/medium algorithm problem.  
- Review one flashcard of key data structures or algorithms.

## Midday (30 min)  
- Read or watch a short system design case study or architecture article.  
- Summarize key takeaways in your own words.

## Evening (30 min)  
- Practice one behavioral question using the STAR method.  
- Reflect on the day: note what went well and areas to improve.

Consistency beats cramming—stick to this routine to build steady momentum.
