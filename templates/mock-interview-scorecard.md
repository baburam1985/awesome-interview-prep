# Mock Interview Scorecard

Use this scorecard to evaluate your performance in mock interviews. For each category, assign a rating (e.g., 1–5) and record detailed notes.

## Categories  
- **Coding correctness**: Did you produce working, bug‑free code? Note any mistakes or corrections.
- **Problem‑solving approach**: How effectively did you break down the problem and explain your thought process? Did you consider trade‑offs?
- **System design depth**: (if applicable) Did you identify key components, scalability concerns, and trade‑offs?
- **Communication clarity**: Were your explanations clear and structured? Did you actively listen and ask clarifying questions?
- **Leadership and collaboration**: Did you demonstrate ownership and a teamwork mindset?
- **Cultural fit**: Did your stories align with the company’s values and mission?
- **Overall impression**: Summarize the overall strength of the interview, highlighting areas for improvement and what went well.

For each category, record:
- **Rating** (1–5)
- **Notes**: Key observations, strengths, areas for improvement, and follow‑up actions.
