# Behavioral Interview Stories Template

Use this template to structure your behavioral interview stories using the **STAR** method (Situation, Task, Action, Result).

## Situation  
Describe the context or background of the experience. Set the scene for the interviewer so they understand the environment and stakeholders.

## Task  
Explain the specific task, challenge, or responsibility you had in that situation.

## Action  
Detail the actions you personally took to address the task. Focus on your decision‑making process, the steps you followed, and how you collaborated with others. Highlight any trade‑offs you considered.

## Result  
Share the outcome of your actions. Quantify the impact where possible (e.g., reduced latency by 40%, increased revenue by $1M). Reflect on lessons learned and how they have influenced your approach since.
